# Patterns & Techniques: Ahrens' Smart Notes

## The Standard Workflow
**When to use**: Whenever you are reading, researching, or thinking about a topic you want to remember or write about.
**How**:
1. Take **fleeting notes** during the day.
2. Take **literature notes** while reading (keep them brief, focus on the gist).
3. Translate the above into **permanent notes** (one idea per note, your own words).
4. **Link** the new note to at least one existing note in the slip-box.
5. Periodically check for **clusters** and turn them into manuscripts.

## Elaborative Interrogation
**When to use**: While reading a difficult text or trying to integrate a new idea.
**How**: Ask yourself:
- "How does this connect to [Existing Note]?"
- "What does this contradict?"
- "What is a concrete example of this abstract principle?"
- "What is missing from this argument?"

## Branching (Luhmann Style)
**When to use**: When you want to add a comment or a related thought to a note without interrupting the main sequence.
**How**:
- If you have note `12`, and you want to expand on it, create `12a`.
- If `12a` needs expansion, create `12a1` or `12b`.
- This allows for "infinite" internal growth.

## Slip-box-storming
**When to use**: When you need to find a topic for a paper or article.
**How**:
1. Open your slip-box and look for the largest clusters of notes.
2. Follow the links between notes in that cluster to see the "thread."
3. Use those notes as the building blocks for your outline.
4. Let the cluster's weight determine the paper's importance.

## Kill Your Darlings (with a Rest-Document)
**When to use**: During the final drafting phase when you have too much interesting material.
**How**:
1. Create a document called `[Project]-Rest.doc`.
2. Cut every passage that doesn't fit the main argument.
3. Paste it into the Rest document.
4. This removes the psychological pain of "deleting" work, making it easier to stay focused.

## The 3-Note Daily Minimum
**When to use**: To build the habit and reach critical mass.
**How**: Commit to writing at least 3 permanent notes every single day, no matter how small. This ensures the box grows steadily.
