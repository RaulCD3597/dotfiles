# Cheatsheet: Make It Stick

## The Golden Rules of Learning
1. **Durable learning is effortful.** If it's easy, it's probably not sticking.
2. **Retrieval is everything.** Practice fetching it from memory, don't just look at it.
3. **Space it out.** Let yourself forget a little before you try to retrieve.
4. **Mix it up.** Don't practice the same thing over and over; shuffle your topics.

## Decision Matrix: How to Study
| If your goal is... | Don't... | Instead, DO... |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Long-term memory** | Reread, highlight | Self-quiz, use flashcards |
| **Versatile skill** | Massed practice | Interleave & Vary practice |
| **Deep understanding** | Copy notes | Elaborate & use Metaphors |
| **Accurate judgment** | Trust your gut | Calibrate via testing |
| **Solving new problems**| Memorize examples | Abstract the underlying Rules |

## Quick Self-Calibration Check
Ask yourself these 3 questions after every study session:
1. **What are the 3 most important ideas?** (Retrieval)
2. **How does this relate to what I already know?** (Elaboration)
3. **What is a real-world example of this?** (Application)

## Mnemonic Strategy: The Memory Palace
1. **Select Place**: Use your home or office.
2. **Define Path**: Entry → Hall → Kitchen → Stairs...
3. **Station Images**: Put a "giant brain" on the stove to remember "Metacognition."
4. **Retrieve**: "Walk" the path in your mind to see the images.

## Growth Mindset Calibration
- **Instead of**: "I'm not good at this."
- **Say**: "I haven't mastered this *yet*, but effortful practice will build the neural pathways I need."
- **Instead of**: "That test was too hard."
- **Say**: "That test revealed exactly where my mental model is weak. Now I know where to focus."

## Key Ratios
- **60/40 Rule**: Spend 60% of study time in recitation/quizzing and only 40% in reading.
- **The 24/7/30 Rule**: Review within 24 hours, after 7 days, and then after 30 days.
